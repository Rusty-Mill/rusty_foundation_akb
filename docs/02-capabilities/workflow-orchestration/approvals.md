# Approvals, quorum, and separation of duties

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0001:** Approval policy binds exact request/resource/effect generation, eligible actors, independence/SoD constraints, quorum/threshold/order, evidence visible, allowed decisions, expiry, withdrawal, delegation, escalation, and changed-context revalidation.

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0002:** Authentication, task assignment, opening content, submitting a decision, quorum satisfaction, downstream fulfillment, and target effect remain separate milestones.

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0003:** Quorum counts unique eligible independent actors under the named policy, detects aliases/delegates/conflicts, preserves dissent/abstention/nonresponse, and cannot reuse one decision across changed request generations.

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0004:** Self-approval, requestor/beneficiary/owner/administrator conflicts, toxic role combinations, prior-stage participation, and emergency exceptions are evaluated before each decision and again before fulfillment.

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0005:** Approvers receive minimized sufficient evidence, provenance/freshness/unknowns, consequences, alternatives, and accessible time; workflow design avoids dark patterns, coerced defaults, misleading urgency, and hidden scope.

**RM-WORKFLOW-APPROVAL-0006:** Approval withdrawal or policy/evidence change before effect invalidates or re-evaluates fulfillment; after effect it initiates revocation/compensation/reconciliation rather than rewriting history.
