# Human tasks, assignments, and forms

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0001:** A human task binds task/instance/definition generation, semantic type, subject/resource/action, input/form schema, candidate and assigned principals/groups, claimant, authority, privacy, priority, created/due/expiry, escalation, and completion policy.

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0002:** Available, offered, assigned, claimed, opened, saved, submitted, decided, completed, delegated, escalated, withdrawn, expired, cancelled, and failed are distinct revisioned states.

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0003:** Claim, release, delegate, reassign, save, submit, approve, reject, request-change, withdraw, and comment are typed commands with current revision, actor, authorization, idempotency, and audit.

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0004:** Forms bind schema/UI generation, localized resources, validation, accessible semantics, data purpose/classification, attachment constraints, drafts, signatures/attestations, and exact submitted immutable evidence.

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0005:** Task-list filtering protects task existence, subject/resource metadata, counts, priority, deadlines, and snippets and rechecks authorization on claim/open/submit/decision.

**RM-WORKFLOW-TASK-0006:** Absence, substitution, delegation, reassignment, escalation, shared queues, mobile/offline work, conflicting edits, and deadline changes preserve assignment provenance and never fabricate the original assignee's decision.
