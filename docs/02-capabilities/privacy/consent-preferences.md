# Consent, preferences, and withdrawal

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0001:** A consent offer binds controller/product identity, subject, precise purposes/actions/data/recipients, required versus optional consequences, duration, withdrawal method/effect, policy/notice text generation, locale/accessibility, and presentation channel.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0002:** A grant binds offer digest/generation, subject and granting principal/authority, affirmative choices per granular purpose, presentation/interaction evidence, time/clock, source/device/session, expiry, and exclusions. Prechecked, bundled, coerced, ambiguous, or silent interaction is represented and rejected when policy requires affirmative choice.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0003:** Platform permission, application consent, marketing preference, cookie/storage choice, contract acceptance, terms acknowledgment, biometric authorization, and product feature activation are different evidence and cannot substitute for one another without policy mapping.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0004:** Withdrawal is a new authenticated/authorized event that is as accessible as granting, applies prospectively under selected policy, invalidates future consent-dependent operations, and triggers scoped reconciliation of queued processing, subscriptions, sharing, derived uses, caches, and providers.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0005:** Withdrawal cannot undo prior disclosure or required retained evidence. Results name effective time, operations stopped, data restricted/erased/retained, recipients notified, exceptions/holds, propagation, and residuals.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0006:** Preference signals bind source, subject/browser/account/device scope, signal specification/version, authenticity/context, purpose/category mapping, time/expiry, conflict precedence, and downstream propagation. A signal is policy evidence, not a universal legal command.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONSENT-0007:** Consent receipts and preference stores are sensitive, integrity-protected, purpose-limited, minimised, retained, exported, corrected, and erased under explicit policy; they never embed unnecessary content or behavioral history.
