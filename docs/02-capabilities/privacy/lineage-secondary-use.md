# Lineage, derivation, and secondary use

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0001:** Privacy lineage links each output generation to exact parent data, selected fields/rows/ranges, subject linkage, purpose and plan, operation/provider/model/code generations, recipients/locations, semantic loss, and resulting classification/retention.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0002:** Lineage must support purpose enforcement and rights discovery without itself becoming an unrestricted identity graph. It uses opaque references, projections, access controls, partitioning, retention, and anti-reidentification policy.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0003:** Derived scores, embeddings, features, profiles, predictions, segments, recommendations, aggregates, synthetic data, and model parameters are independently assessed for subject linkage, reversibility, memorization, inference, group impact, accuracy, and rights applicability under product policy.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0004:** Secondary-use requests bind original/new purpose plans, compatible-use policy, affected subjects/data, derived outputs, recipients, risks/safeguards, notice/choice requirements, reviewer, rollout, and stop/rollback/reconciliation path.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0005:** Join/linkage across tenants, datasets, devices, contexts, or pseudonyms requires separately authorized linkage policy and records match method/confidence, false-link risk, subject expectations, and unlink/correction effects.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0006:** Lineage gaps, unknown processors, opaque models, lossy exports, deleted parents, or unverifiable identifiers yield incomplete coverage and safe policy outcomes; a missing edge cannot prove that data is outside a rights request.

**RM-PRIVACY-LINEAGE-0007:** Deleting or restricting a parent triggers product-defined treatment of descendants—erase, recompute, detach, aggregate, restrict, retain under hold, or review—with exact residual and semantic-correctness evidence.
