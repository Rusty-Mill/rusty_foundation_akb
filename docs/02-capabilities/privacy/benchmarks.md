# Benchmarks

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0001:** Plan/enforcement workloads vary subjects/data elements/actions/purposes, schema projection, identity/tenant, policy generations, grants/preferences, recipients/processors/regions, storage providers, cache state, concurrency, and failover while checking exact allowed data flow before latency.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0002:** Rights workloads vary request type, identity/agent verification, subject aliases/accounts/devices, stores/replicas/indexes/caches/recipients/processors, result volume, third-party redaction, export format/protection, corrections, holds, erasure methods, backups/models, and parallel cases.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0003:** Measure time to acknowledge/verify/discover/decide/communicate, export goodput, correction/erasure/restriction propagation and convergence, residual counts/age, deadline headroom, provider calls/costs, CPU/memory/storage/network, queue/human review, retries, failures, and privacy-system data collected.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0004:** Retention trials measure inventory evaluation, expiry scheduling, batch deletion, cryptographic erasure, tombstone creation/use, index/cache/replica/recipient convergence, backup expiry and restore suppression, model retrain/unlearning, storage reclaimed, and verification cost.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0005:** Deidentification workloads vary population/distribution, dimensionality/sparsity, linkage, algorithm/parameters, privacy budget/composition, releases/queries, utility metrics, adversary/auxiliary data, concurrency, and hardware while reporting risk and semantic correctness before throughput.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0006:** Adversarial trials measure bounded behavior under subject enumeration, forged/replayed grants, identity collisions, massive lineage, recipient/processor churn, region loss, repeated/overlapping requests, malformed exports, hold conflicts, deletion resurrection, provider outages, and audit/export exfiltration attempts.

**RM-PRIVACY-BENCH-0007:** Reports use synthetic or approved deidentified test data, disclose hardware/OS/provider/topology/regions, policy and inventory generations, workload distribution, safeguards, cache/warmup, concurrency/resources, repetitions/uncertainty, telemetry, user/reviewer time, monetary cost, and known coverage limits.
