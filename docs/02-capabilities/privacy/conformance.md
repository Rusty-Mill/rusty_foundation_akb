**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0002:** Consent/preference histories cover granular accept/refuse, required/optional service consequences, accessibility/localization, minors/agents under product policy, platform permission distinction, multiple devices/accounts, conflicts, expiry, withdrawal, queued/offline processing, downstream propagation, and dark-pattern assertions.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0003:** Inventory/lineage suites trace collection through stores, replicas, caches, indexes, messages, exports, analytics, logs, backups, processors, models, joins, pseudonyms, aggregates, deletion/correction, migration, failover, rebuild, restore, and unknown frontiers.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0004:** Transfer suites vary roles/entities/tenants/recipients/subprocessors, logical/physical/support/key/telemetry regions, federation/contract/policy changes, cross-region failure, onward transfer, emergency failover/repatriation, recipient deletion, and evidence gaps.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0005:** Rights-case histories cover intake, anti-enumeration, identity and agents, scope clarification, deadlines/extensions, discovery, third-party segregation, access/export/correction/restriction/objection/erasure, holds/exceptions, processors, communication, appeal, withdrawal, duplicate/abuse, and closure residuals.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0007:** Deidentification suites evaluate direct/quasi identifiers, auxiliary joins, rare groups, temporal/location trails, differencing/composition, membership/inference/reconstruction, token/key compromise, privacy-budget accounting, utility/loss, new releases, and controlled reidentification testing.

**RM-PRIVACY-CONFORMANCE-0008:** Reports bind datasets/corpora and synthetic subject digests, product/jurisdiction policy generations, purposes/plans, identity/tenant/roles, systems/providers/regions, inventories/lineage coverage, clocks/calendars, limits, expected histories, privacy safeguards, and every skipped/degraded assertion without production personal data.
