# Cross-cutting qualities

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0001:** Security review covers subject enumeration/impersonation, agent/guardian fraud, purpose-token forgery/replay, consent spoofing/coercion, preference loss, overcollection, linkage/reidentification, unauthorized secondary use, recipient/processor drift, residency failover, rights-export leakage, erasure bypass/resurrection, audit misuse, and insider access.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0002:** Privacy itself applies to privacy-system data: notices/choices, consent receipts, subject graphs, request cases, identity evidence, search queries, exports, correspondence, exceptions/holds, recipient lists, lineage, audits, risk scores, and complaints use minimization, access, retention, erasure, and anti-reidentification.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0003:** Accessibility provides localized plain-language notices and choices, granular keyboard/screen-reader controls, equivalent refusal/withdrawal/request paths, progress and deadlines, secure export access, correction/appeal, authorized-agent support, and no dark patterns or retaliation mechanics.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0004:** Internationalization keeps stable policy/purpose IDs separate from translations and handles jurisdictions, civil calendars, time zones, names/addresses, scripts, right-to-left layouts, culturally appropriate identity verification, locale-specific data categories, and cross-border recipients.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0005:** Observability reports plan/policy generations, actual data actions/projections/recipients/regions, grants/withdrawals, rights stages, inventory coverage, retention/erasure propagation, provider outcomes, limits, and residuals with privacy-preserving identifiers and field-level controls.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0006:** Resource bounds cover subject graph and inventory size, data stores/recipients/processors, lineage depth, discovery queries/results, exports, corrections/erasure fanout, backups/models, provider calls, case concurrency, CPU/memory/storage/network/time, diagnostics, and human-review queues.

**RM-PRIVACY-XCUT-0007:** Multi-tenant isolation covers purpose/policy, identities/subjects, grants/preferences, data/lineage, recipient/processor routes, rights cases, exports, holds, privacy budgets, audit, keys/tokens, providers, and support access; aggregation never crosses tenants without explicit authority and risk policy.
