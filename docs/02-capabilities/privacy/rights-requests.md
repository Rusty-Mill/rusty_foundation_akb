# Rights requests and case workflow

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0001:** Request types include know/access, categories/sources/purposes/recipients, export/portability, correction, deletion/erasure, restriction, objection/opt-out/limit, consent withdrawal, automated-decision review, appeal, and complaint under product-selected policy.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0002:** A case binds requester and subject/agent authority evidence, requested rights and scope, jurisdiction/policy generation, channels/language/accessibility, received/deadline clocks, identity-verification level, affected controllers/processors/tenants, status, communications, decisions, effects, residuals, and appeal.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0003:** Intake acknowledges receipt without confirming record existence, minimizes collection, supports accessible/non-digital or authorized-agent routes where product policy selects them, rate-limits abuse, and preserves anti-retaliation/non-discrimination evidence where applicable.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0004:** Discovery uses authenticated subject references plus lineage and system inventories, records query/provider generations and coverage, deduplicates without losing provenance, and isolates other subjects, secrets, privileged/confidential data, security evidence, and third-party rights.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0005:** Decision separates identity insufficient, request clarified, fulfilled, partially fulfilled, refused, exempted/held, excessive/duplicate under selected policy, deadline extended, withdrawn, cancelled, and appealed with issuer-qualified reasons and review authority.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0006:** Deadlines use civil time, jurisdiction/calendar rules, receipt/verification/pause/extension events, clock uncertainty, notification channels, and evidence. Scheduling cannot determine the legal deadline without a product policy artifact.

**RM-PRIVACY-RIGHTS-0007:** Case closure requires communication delivery evidence, effect reconciliation, processor/recipient follow-up, export expiry/cleanup, outstanding residuals, appeal path, and retained audit under explicit policy; a ticket status alone is insufficient.
