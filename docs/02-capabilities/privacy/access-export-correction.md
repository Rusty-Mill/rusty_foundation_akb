# Access, export, correction, and portability

**RM-PRIVACY-ACCESS-0001:** Access responses distinguish raw values, intelligible representation, categories, sources, purposes/actions, recipients/categories, retention, decisions/inferences, rights/policy information, and provenance; products select required projections and explanations.

**RM-PRIVACY-ACCESS-0002:** Response construction applies third-party segregation/redaction, secrets/credentials/security protection, privilege/confidentiality policy, minimization, localization, accessibility, format/schema/version, integrity, pagination, size limits, and human review.

**RM-PRIVACY-EXPORT-0001:** Export packages bind case/subject/tenant, scope and discovery snapshot, item schemas/media, source and export generations, omissions/redactions, timestamps/units/encodings, relationships, checksums/digests, optional encryption/signature, expiry, and safe delivery authority.

**RM-PRIVACY-EXPORT-0002:** Portability additionally declares machine-readable format/profile, semantic field mapping, identity/reference stability, unknown/loss handling, receiving-controller route, transfer authority, receipt, cancellation, retry, and no inference of import compatibility or downstream deletion.

**RM-PRIVACY-CORRECT-0001:** Correction requests bind target record/field and generation, asserted value/evidence, subject scope, authoritative source, validation, dependent derivatives/decisions, concurrency, approval, and rollback/reconciliation plan.

**RM-PRIVACY-CORRECT-0002:** Correction preserves historical/audit evidence only where policy permits, marks disputed/uncertain values, updates or invalidates indexes/caches/profiles/models/recipients, and reports systems that cannot or must not change.

**RM-PRIVACY-ACCESS-0003:** Exports and communications are sensitive artifacts with least-privilege access, strong recipient authentication, bounded download, encryption where selected, no predictable links, expiry/revocation, audit, and deletion.
