# Conformance

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0001:** Decision suites cover every typed request/result, principal/resource/action/context combination, missing/null/unknown/error/not-applicable/indeterminate, schema mismatch, default, reason, obligation/advice, and enforcement mapping.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0002:** Language suites use golden parse/type/value/function/operator/collection/entity/quantifier/recursion/error/unknown/resource vectors across implementations and optimization/compiled/interpreted paths.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0003:** Composition suites exhaust truth/error tables, precedence/order, deny/permit overrides, only/first applicable, conflicts, multiple results, obligation/advice merging, delegated attenuation, and mandatory-domain interaction.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0004:** Data/cache suites inject stale/missing/partial/unauthorized/entity-cycle data, time/risk changes, cache collisions/expiry/invalidation, nondeterministic functions, partial/residual evaluation, offline state, and emergency revocation.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0005:** Enforcement suites race principal/resource/policy changes and fail decision delivery, unsupported/pre/in/post obligations, transactions/fencing/idempotency, duplicate/restart, partial effects, compensation, and audit correlation.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0006:** Distribution suites test invalid/mixed/rollback/equivocating bundles, schema/function incompatibility, signatures/revocation, received-to-serving acknowledgments, progressive/shadow activation, last-known-good expiry, rollback, restart, and disaster recovery.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0007:** Testing/explanation/privacy suites verify old/new deltas, historical limitations, static-analysis soundness claims, trace/reason redaction, decision-log sampling/loss, accessibility/localization, and no semantic dependence on diagnostics.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0008:** Security suites fuzz parser/compiler/evaluator/functions/entity graphs, resource exhaustion, dynamic code, namespaces/dependencies, side channels, cross-tenant caches/data, break-glass, hostile updates, and cleanup.

**RM-POLICY-CONFORMANCE-0009:** Provider reports publish unsupported semantics, extensions/emulations, weaker guarantees, configuration/version prerequisites, performance/cost, validation/analysis limitations, and waivers.
