# Distribution, activation, and rollback

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0001:** Policy artifacts package immutable modules, schemas, data/manifests, function dependencies, metadata, source/content identities, compatibility, signatures/provenance, validation results, and activation policy.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0002:** Publication separates author, review, approve, sign, publish, promote, activate, rollback, revoke, and emergency authority with quorum for high-impact policy.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0003:** Consumers authenticate and validate complete coherent policy/schema/data/function bundles before activation; files/modules from different generations cannot be mixed.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0004:** Acknowledgments distinguish received, signature/structure validated, compiled/static-valid, locally accepted, activated, serving decisions, evaluated samples, and observed enforcement outcomes.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0005:** Progressive activation defines target fleet/tenant/workload, old/new compatibility, shadow/simulation, traffic percentage, guardrails, pause/rollback, mixed-generation limit, completion, and retirement.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0006:** Last-known-good has maximum age and explicit fail-closed/open behavior per decision domain; emergency revocation and critical security updates override stale cache policy.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0007:** Rollback selects a newly authorized coherent generation and reevaluates schema/data/function compatibility; it is not proof that historical policy is currently safe or legal.

**RM-POLICY-DIST-0008:** Backup/restore/failover preserves immutable artifacts, signatures, namespace/allocation history, approval/audit, aliases/channels, revocations, and rollback protection.
