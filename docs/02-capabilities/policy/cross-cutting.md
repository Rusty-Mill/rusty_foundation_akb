# Cross-cutting qualities

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0001:** Secure defaults are schema validation, immutable authenticated bundles, pure bounded evaluation, explicit unknown/error/default, fail-closed security decisions, no ambient functions, enforced critical obligations, and generation-bound reuse.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0002:** Performance budgets cover parse/validate/compile, data acquisition, evaluation, partial evaluation, cache, explanation/logging, distribution/activation, memory/CPU/network, cold/warm, tail/adversarial work, and provider cost without weakening decisions silently.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0003:** Accessibility exposes decisions, requirements/obligations, denial/error/recovery, approvals and simulations through semantic keyboard-operable interfaces, readable localized reasons, non-color state, reduced motion, and controllable updates.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0004:** Internationalization keeps policy identifiers and evaluation locale-neutral unless locale/collation/language is explicit input, validates Unicode normalization/case rules, and separates translated explanations/docs from decision semantics.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0005:** Observability records generation-bound evaluation/decision/enforcement milestones, decision classes, missing/error/cost/cache/partial state, rollout propagation, obligation outcomes, resource use, and causal context with privacy and cardinality controls.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0006:** Metrics distinguish request validation, evaluation, decision delivery, enforcement, obligation, and domain effect; permit/deny rates lack meaning without entry point/population/generation and do not become success/security metrics alone.

**RM-POLICY-XCUT-0007:** Shutdown/upgrade drains evaluations and data fetches, atomically switches coherent bundles, preserves last-known-good/revocation policy, completes or reconciles obligations, flushes required audit, and bounds cleanup.
