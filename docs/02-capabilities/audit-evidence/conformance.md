# Conformance

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0001:** Schema suites cover every event class/boundary/outcome, required/forbidden/classified fields, identity generations, unknown evolution, canonicalization, hostile values, secret canaries, and platform/provider mappings.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0002:** Capture histories crash/fail every point before/during/after domain effect, outbox, append receipt, durable segment, proof, index, export, and reconciliation; they verify honest ambiguous/lost/duplicate/orphan states and fail-mode policy.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0003:** Sequence/time suites cover multiple sources/partitions, restart/clone/rollback, gaps/duplicates/reorder/backfill, clock skew/step/unknown quality, trace/workflow causality, signed empty intervals, and qualified completeness reports.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0004:** Integrity suites mutate/delete/reorder/truncate/insert/fork segments and proofs; rotate/revoke/lose/compromise keys; break canonicalization/anchors/timestamps; validate partial ranges; and prove that capture omissions remain outside integrity claims.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0005:** Privacy/retention suites cover cross-tenant queries, token collision/rotation, reidentification, redaction/suppression, subject access/correction/erasure, holds, cryptographic erasure, backups/restore, index/cache purge, and proof survivability.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0006:** Assessment/report suites cover scope/population/sample, control versions, evidence lineage, findings/risks/exceptions, assessor independence, continuous indicators, expiry/supersession/withdrawal, accessible exports, and no blanket compliance claim.

**RM-AUDIT-CONFORMANCE-0007:** Evidence records fixtures/seeds, domain/source/schema/policy/collection/storage/proof/key/trust/index/export/tool generations, clocks/frontiers/fault schedule, privacy view, expected populations, results/gaps/nonclaims, and artifact digests.
