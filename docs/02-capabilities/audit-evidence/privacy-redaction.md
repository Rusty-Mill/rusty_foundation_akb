# Privacy, redaction, tokenization, and disclosure

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0001:** Audit schemas minimize personal/content data at capture and use stable subject/resource references, typed categories, safe reason codes, and separately protected evidence artifacts instead of verbose payloads.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0002:** Raw, tokenized/pseudonymized, redacted, aggregated, investigation, subject-access, regulator/auditor, and public transparency views are versioned purpose- and audience-specific projections with loss reports.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0003:** Tokenization binds namespace/tenant/purpose/key generation, determinism/linkability scope, rotation, collision, reidentification authority, and deletion. Hashing a small identifier space is not anonymization.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0004:** Redaction/suppression records identify source field/artifact, method, reason, authority, effective scope, reversible/irreversible state, residual inference, and proof impact without exposing removed content.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0005:** Search, export, correlation, joins, drill-down, and bulk analysis enforce purpose, tenant, row/field, time, case, approval, and result-size controls and audit the access.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0006:** Subject access/correction/erasure workflows distinguish auditable facts, mutable annotations, protected third-party/security/legal evidence, pseudonymous references, holds, and disclosure limits under reviewed policy.

**RM-AUDIT-PRIVACY-0007:** Small populations, rare event types, exact times, IP/device/location, free text, and relationship graphs receive inference and reidentification controls in dashboards and reports.
