# Query, investigation, export, and reporting

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0001:** Query contracts define event/evidence schema generations, tenant/case/purpose authorization, time and sequence ranges, predicates, joins/correlation, point-in-time snapshot/frontier, sort/total order, pagination, limits, and completeness.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0002:** Results disclose index/source frontier, known gaps/backfill, late/corrected/suppressed records, projection/redaction, approximate aggregation, clock uncertainty, and verification status.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0003:** Investigation workspaces bind case, investigators/roles, approved scope/purpose, saved queries, notes/hypotheses, evidence references, chain of custody, exports, decisions, retention/hold, and closure.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0004:** Export manifests bind exact query/snapshot, records/artifacts/proofs, schema and mapping, canonical digests, encryption/recipient key, signer, creation/expiry, chain of custody, transfer/receipt, and redaction.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0005:** Reports bind definition/version, system/control/population scope, period/frontiers, source evidence, transformations/calculations, exceptions/unknowns, reviewer/approver, issue/expiry, and reproducible artifact digest.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0006:** Dashboards, counts, timelines, and anomaly indicators are derived views and never silently exclude unknown/unindexed/suppressed populations or convert correlation into causation.

**RM-AUDIT-QUERY-0007:** Human-facing reports are accessible, localizable where appropriate, use stable machine identifiers plus readable labels, and provide alternatives for charts, integrity proofs, and complex evidence graphs.
