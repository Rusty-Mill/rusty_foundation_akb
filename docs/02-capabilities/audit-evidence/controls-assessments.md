# Controls, assessments, findings, and attestations

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0001:** Control definitions bind framework/profile/version, stable control/objective ID, applicability, implementation responsibility, assessment methods/objects, evidence requirements, frequency, tolerances, exceptions, and supersession.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0002:** Control implementation statement, design review, test procedure, observation, evidence, finding, risk, remediation, exception/acceptance, continuous signal, and attestation are separate entities.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0003:** Assessment plans bind system boundary/inventory/configuration generation, controls/objectives, period, assessor identity/independence, methods/tools/versions, samples/populations, rules of engagement, evidence access, and limitations.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0004:** Assessment results preserve actual procedures, tested objects/populations, evidence references, observations, findings and risks, false-positive/disposition, deviations, uncertainty, dates, assessor, review, and expiry.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0005:** Continuous-control indicators name source, control relation, calculation, threshold, freshness, coverage, failure/gap behavior, and review; a green metric cannot replace periodic judgment beyond its scope.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0006:** Attestations are signed, scoped, dated claims by named responsible parties over immutable report/system/control/evidence generations with qualifications, reliance limits, expiry, withdrawal/supersession, and verification.

**RM-AUDIT-CONTROL-0007:** Rusty Mill supports mappings and evidence but never declares legal/regulatory/certification compliance without product-selected frameworks, qualified assessment, and accountable approval.
