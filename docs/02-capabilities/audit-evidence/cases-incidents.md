# Incident, case, and workflow linkage

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0001:** Incident, investigation, privacy-rights, fraud, dispute, legal, compliance, and support cases remain typed workflows with separate authority, confidentiality, deadlines, evidence, and disclosure policy.

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0002:** Linking an event/artifact to a case records linker, reason, scope, time, authority, confidence, and provenance; a link does not prove the case hypothesis or event culpability.

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0003:** Evidence collection plans identify sources/frontiers, preservation method, volatile data, minimization, custodians, acquisition tool/version, integrity checks, chain of custody, legal/organizational authority, and known loss.

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0004:** Notes, labels, indicators, timelines, hypotheses, conclusions, remediation, and disclosures are appended/superseded with authorship and review and remain distinguishable from captured source evidence.

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0005:** Restricted-case access, sealed fields, need-to-know compartments, conflict-of-interest, dual control, break-glass, export approval, and post-access review compose application authorization and audit themselves.

**RM-AUDIT-CASE-0006:** Closing a case records disposition, unresolved unknowns, preservation/retention changes, remediation links, notifications/disclosures, lessons learned, and later reopen/supersession policy.
