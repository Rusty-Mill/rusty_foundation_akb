## Ownership duties

The owner maintains authority-kind compatibility, multidimensional subset proof, unrepresentable/incomparable failure, ambient non-escalation, provenance/inspection, close/duplicate/transfer/delegation/revocation lifecycle, A-claim vectors, dependency/service composition, source, quality, conformance, benchmark, and capability-readiness semantics. Provider owners maintain separate native mechanisms and bypass/deployment frontiers; consuming operations/services retain enforcement and product policy.

## Bounded trial plan

A later disposable trial may exercise instrumented logical authorities plus one bounded native authority kind per platform, deriving across operation/resource/lifetime/audience/delegation dimensions, rejecting every expansion/incomparable input, inspecting redacted provenance, racing close/derive, transferring with accept/reject/cancel, and testing scoped revocation/aliases/in-flight operations. Claimed A-vectors receive exact bypass probes; restricted execution is tested only under its separately authorized service scope.

The trial uses the [foundation trial template](../../05-governance/implementation-trials/trial-template.md), synthetic nonsensitive resources/principals/policies, disposable processes/containers/accounts where authorized, least privilege, hard lifetime/resource bounds, isolated native code, no production credentials/resources, no host-wide policy mutation, and no release publication. It does not select public Rust APIs, authority serialization, policy language, sandbox product, elevation/authentication, cross-machine delegation, universal revocation, provider matrix, performance budgets, or production support.

Stop conditions include any authority expansion, silently dropped constraint, ambient grant, wrong-resource/operation use, credential/policy-material leakage, parent mutation on failure, alias/delegation escape, misleading revocation success, A-claim inflation, unrestricted child release, host/session/policy risk, leaked native authority/process, provenance loss, or material source/contract/environment drift.
