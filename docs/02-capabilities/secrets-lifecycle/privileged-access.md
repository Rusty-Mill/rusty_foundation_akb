# Privileged checkout and break-glass

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0001:** Privileged checkout binds requester/beneficiary/actor, target/account, purpose/ticket/incident, requested actions, authentication/assurance, device/context, approvals/quorum, start/expiry, interaction, recording policy, and authority.

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0002:** Just-in-time access prefers brokered session or dynamic credential with narrow target/action/lifetime over revealing a standing shared secret. Checkout never grants ownership or unrestricted redelegation.

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0003:** Dual control and quorum validate independent eligible approvers, prevent self-approval and reused identities, bind exact operation and generation, expire, and record refusal/unavailability without revealing secret material.

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0004:** Session brokerage keeps target credentials outside the operator endpoint where possible, authenticates both parties, binds protocol and target, restricts transfer/file/clipboard/command features, and separates session recording from keystroke/content privacy policy.

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0005:** Break-glass mechanisms define offline availability, custody, scope, bypasses, activation evidence, notification, maximum lifetime, target validation, post-use rotation, reconciliation, and after-action review.

**RM-SECRETS-PRIV-0006:** Emergency access does not bypass native target authorization, safety interlocks, immutable ledger controls, or product-defined actions outside the declared incident scope.
