# User mediation, policy tips, and justification

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0001:** Mediation binds operation and subject snapshot, exact policy decision, risk and affected recipients/channels, required action, available alternatives, deadline, interaction principal/session, and accessibility/localization context.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0002:** Informational labels, recommendations, mandatory choices, policy tips, warnings, justifications, approvals, blocks, errors, and incident notices are distinct accessible interaction kinds and cannot be disguised as one another.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0003:** UI displays issuer-qualified localized label names, protection state, reason categories, detected sensitive regions only with authorized minimization, scope, recipient/destination, consequences, offline/stale state, and whether override is available.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0004:** Confirmation cannot broaden recipient, destination, purpose, label, or content after display. Any material change creates a new decision and interaction generation.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0005:** Justification forms use structured options, bounded text, keyboard/screen-reader operation, sufficient time, non-color-only signals, no dark patterns, safe cancellation, and privacy notice. Secure content is not echoed into assistive logs or telemetry.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0006:** Headless, background, service, automation, remote, and disconnected sessions never fabricate user consent. They use preauthorized policy, defer to an interaction broker, route for approval, or fail with a typed outcome.

**RM-PROTECTION-UX-0007:** Repeated tips/warnings use stable operation correlation and rate limits without suppressing newly higher risk, changed policy, changed recipient, or changed subject evidence.
