# Audit, privacy, and governance

**RM-PROTECTION-AUDIT-0001:** Audit correlates subject and policy generations, classification evidence, label transition, protection plan/effects, channel decision/effect, principal/recipient/destination, mediation/justification/approval, provider/freshness, incident, and reconciliation without logging sensitive content by default.

**RM-PROTECTION-AUDIT-0002:** Detected values, excerpts, document names/paths, recipients, justifications, classifier features, labels, rights, locations, and user behavior are separately classified and minimized, tokenized/redacted, access-controlled, retained, exported, and erased under policy.

**RM-PROTECTION-AUDIT-0003:** Audit access supports purpose limitation, field projection, separation of duties, legal hold, user/privacy requests, immutable integrity evidence, clock uncertainty, and monitoring for insider misuse.

**RM-PROTECTION-GOVERN-0001:** Governance tracks taxonomy owner, legal/security/privacy/data-owner approvals, label definitions/examples, protection mappings, DLP rules, classifier validation, exception inventory, rollouts, incidents, reviews, and retirement.

**RM-PROTECTION-GOVERN-0002:** Effectiveness measures include coverage, unlabeled/conflicting/stale inventory, recommendation acceptance, false positive/negative samples, overrides and reasons, approval latency, unsupported channels, partial effects, incident escape, user accessibility/usability, and privacy cost.

**RM-PROTECTION-GOVERN-0003:** Automated classifier and DLP changes require versioned evaluation datasets, subgroup/language/format analysis, drift monitoring, adversarial testing, human-review capacity, rollback, and prohibition on unverifiable universal accuracy claims.

**RM-PROTECTION-GOVERN-0004:** Legal/regulatory mappings are jurisdiction-, organization-, purpose-, and time-scoped counsel-approved policy artifacts, not built-in universal Rusty Mill semantics.
