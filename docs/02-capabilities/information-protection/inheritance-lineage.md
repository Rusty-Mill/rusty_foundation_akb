# Inheritance, aggregation, and lineage

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0001:** Derivation operations—copy, query, join, aggregate, export, render, print, capture, transcode, redact, tokenize, archive, extract, message attachment, backup, restore, and AI/model processing—declare classification propagation rules over exact lineage.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0002:** Child-to-parent and parent-to-child propagation are distinct. A container label does not prove every member was inspected or labeled, and a member label does not automatically rewrite an externally signed container.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0003:** Aggregation composes label lattice relations, sensitive-information findings, subject populations, identifiability, minimum group size, linkage/reidentification risk, purpose, and legal policy; aggregation is not automatically less sensitive.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0004:** Redaction, masking, anonymization, pseudonymization, tokenization, encryption, summarization, and synthetic generation create derived subjects with independent reidentification/residual-risk evidence; none automatically declassifies.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0005:** Lineage records parent generations, selected regions/fields/rows, operation/provider/policy generations, semantic loss, output digest/generation, and preserved/strengthened/proposed labels without embedding prohibited source content.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0006:** Conflicting multiple-source labels use an explicit join rule and preserve contributing assertions. A total-order maximum is allowed only for compatible labels in the same declared ordering domain.

**RM-PROTECTION-INHERIT-0007:** Unknown or broken lineage triggers safe fallback, review, or quarantine under policy; absence of lineage cannot prove eligibility for downgrade or external sharing.
