# Sharing, tenants, and external recipients

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0001:** Recipient evidence distinguishes resolved internal identity, guest, federated external identity, consumer identity, group/dynamic group, link audience, anonymous/public, service principal, device, application, unresolved address, and future membership.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0002:** Cross-tenant sharing binds sender and recipient tenants, federation/trust generations, taxonomy mappings, protection/rights interoperability, identity proof, device/application conditions, geography, purpose, expiry, onward-sharing policy, and revocation path.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0003:** Foreign labels are preserved with issuer identity. Mapping may produce equivalent, stronger, weaker-with-approval, unmapped/review, or rejected; matching display names never establish equivalence.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0004:** Group membership and link audiences are snapshot/lease evidence with expansion risk. Policy may require exact recipients, bounded organizations/domains, approval, or revalidation at access rather than send time.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0005:** Sending protected content does not prove recipient provisioning, key/rights availability, successful access, downstream enforcement, deletion, or revocation. Delivery and usage evidence remain separate and privacy constrained.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0006:** External transformation to an interoperable format creates a new artifact with label/protection/marking/loss mapping and cannot silently remove unsupported controls.

**RM-PROTECTION-SHARE-0007:** Tenant migration, merger/split, domain change, issuer retirement, guest conversion, and federation loss use staged mapping and re-protection plans with coexistence, recovery, and audit.
