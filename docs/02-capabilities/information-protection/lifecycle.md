# Offline, freshness, revocation, and reconciliation

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0001:** Label/taxonomy, classifier, policy, recipient/group, device/compliance, trust/federation, rights template, key, revocation, and provider evidence have independent generations and freshness/expiry.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0002:** Offline policy declares available labels, cached rights, maximum staleness, allowed actions/channels/recipients, required local inspection, pending synchronization, clock uncertainty, and fail behavior by classification.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0003:** An offline permit is scoped evidence, not durable authority. Reconnection reconciles current policy, subject generation, label/protection state, recipients, shares, queued effects, overrides, and revocations before further propagation.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0004:** Taxonomy/policy rollout supports validate, simulate, pilot, activate, mixed-generation observation, compatibility window, rollback, emergency revoke, and convergence evidence. Clients never infer activation from download alone.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0005:** Label retirement and policy revocation cannot erase existing artifacts. They define read, relabel/reprotect, restrict, quarantine, migrate, archive, or destroy behavior with coverage and residual inventories.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0006:** Rights revocation names provider, audience, token/license cache, device/application offline lease, propagation objective, observed acknowledgments, exclusions, and residual authorized plaintext; global recall is not claimed.

**RM-PROTECTION-LIFE-0007:** Recovery from partial label/protection/DLP effects reads authoritative content/object/message state and journals, then completes, compensates, re-protects, quarantines, or escalates without assuming reversible disclosure.
