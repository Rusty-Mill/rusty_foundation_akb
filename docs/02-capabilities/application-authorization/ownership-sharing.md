# Ownership, sharing, grants, and denies

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0001:** Ownership is a typed issuer- and resource-scoped relation with powers and duties defined by product policy; it does not inherently bypass tenant, safety, legal hold, mandatory deny, or native enforcement.

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0002:** A grant binds issuer/actor authority, beneficiary or subject set, resource/scope, actions, conditions, purpose, audience, start/expiry, redelegation, provenance, policy generation, and revocation handle.

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0003:** Sharing invitations separate target hint, intended grant, redemption identity, invitation nonce/expiry, inviter authority, and final grant generation. Possession of a forwarded hint does not establish the intended beneficiary.

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0004:** Denies declare issuer, scope, actions, subjects/sets, conditions, precedence, mandatory or discretionary character, expiry, and exception process. Absence of a grant and explicit deny remain distinguishable.

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0005:** Transfer, copy, move, fork, export, link sharing, public access, ownership succession, and orphan handling each define grant/deny inheritance, recipient tenant, downstream control loss, revocation limits, and audit.

**RM-APP-AUTHZ-SHARE-0006:** Revocation prevents future authorization within named propagation boundaries but cannot claw back observed data or committed effects; offline copies and external recipients remain residuals.
