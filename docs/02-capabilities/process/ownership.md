## Ownership duties

The owner maintains launch/argument/environment/stdio, executable-resolution, owned-child, wait/status, control, supervision, pipeline, dependency/profile, source, quality, conformance, benchmark, and promotion semantics. Provider owners maintain separate Windows, Linux, and macOS mappings and exact parser, lifecycle, containment, sandbox/service, and fallback frontiers. Actual promotion and trial records name accountable people and reviewer-independence limitations.

## Bounded trial plan

A later disposable trial may exercise one fixed probe artifact with lossless argument/environment vectors, deny-by-default inheritance inventories, explicit working directory, creation/image/readiness milestones, owned-child wait/control races, executable replacement adversaries, P0–P3 claims where available, and a bounded two- or three-node shell-free pipeline. The matrix includes supported Windows builds, Linux kernels/libcs and namespace/service contexts, and macOS/SDK sandbox/application contexts; unavailable mechanisms are declared rather than emulated silently.

The trial uses the [foundation trial template](../../05-governance/implementation-trials/trial-template.md), synthetic canary values, disposable child artifacts and directories, bounded process/resource quotas, isolated native code, no production credentials/data, no persistent service installation, and no release publication. It does not select public Rust APIs, crates/workspaces, async runtime, shell/terminal abstraction, sandbox product policy, service manager, supported provider matrix, performance budgets, or production supervision.

Stop conditions include ambiguous executable authority, unintended shell/search, cross-launch resource leakage, secret/canary disclosure, PID reuse retargeting, unknown running child, false cancellation/terminal/readiness report, containment or sandbox claim inflation, child escape outside the disposable scope, host/session disruption, unbounded process/endpoint retention, provenance loss, or material source/contract/environment drift.

**RM-PROCESS-OWNER-0001:** Promotion and trial records MUST name accountable people for the domain and every claimed provider/context, exact generations, reviewer independence, and unresolved limitations.

**RM-PROCESS-OWNER-0002:** Trial hypotheses MUST distinguish preparation, creation, image confirmation, readiness, dispatch, terminal observation, reaping, contained-set empty, endpoint drainage, and full reconciliation.

**RM-PROCESS-OWNER-0003:** This bounded plan is evidence only and MUST NOT authorize implementation, unsafe/native interfaces, arbitrary child execution, elevated identity, sandbox escape testing, service installation, packaging, or release.

**RM-PROCESS-OWNER-0004:** Closeout MUST reconcile every child/set/endpoint, remove only verified disposable fixtures, revoke temporary privileges/registrations, account for traces and canaries, retain negative evidence, and exclude trial artifacts from release channels.
