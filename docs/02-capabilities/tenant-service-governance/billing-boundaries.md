# Billing, invoice, payment, and tax boundaries

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0001:** Billing customer/account, tenant, legal entity, payer, invoice recipient, tax registration, payment method, and service beneficiary retain explicit mappings and effective periods.

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0002:** Billing provider records are external observations reconciled by stable provider/account/object/event identities, authenticated webhooks plus periodic source reads, ordering/gap handling, and idempotent state transitions.

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0003:** Rated charge, invoice line, invoice, credit note, payment intent, payment, refund, dispute, tax document, and ledger posting are distinct entities and milestones.

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0004:** Service eligibility under delinquency, payment failure, dispute, refund, invoice finalization, tax hold, or provider outage is explicit product/legal policy and never inferred solely from one provider status.

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0005:** Exports carry billing/charge/usage periods, currency and conversion evidence, provider/service/SKU/resource dimensions, billed/effective/list costs, credits/discounts/tax, source lineage, and reconciliation residuals where applicable.

**RM-TENANT-GOV-BILL-0006:** Rusty Mill supplies evidence and integration boundaries, not tax, revenue-recognition, accounting, consumer-law, or contract conclusions; those require product policy and qualified review.
