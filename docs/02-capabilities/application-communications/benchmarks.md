# Benchmarks

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0001:** Measure audience resolution/eligibility/deduplication, template render/validation, planning, scheduling admission, and time-to-first-attempt by recipients, rules, locales, content complexity, and cache state.

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0002:** Measure per-channel submission latency/throughput/goodput, provider acceptance, callback processing, end-to-end observed delivery, retry/fallback, cancellation, and reconciliation under RTT/loss/throttle/outage.

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0003:** Measure preference/suppression propagation, quiet-hour release, frequency-cap contention, token/endpoint invalidation, webhook backlog, bounce/complaint response, provider failover, and campaign pause/withdraw.

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0004:** Measure MIME/render size, SMS segments, push payload, attachments and link inspection, queue/storage amplification, CPU/memory/network, provider calls/cost, telemetry cardinality, accessibility artifact size, and energy on clients.

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0005:** Sustained/adversarial tests report tail latency, backlog/lag, fairness, duplicate/unknown/loss rates by boundary, suppression violations, provider-limit compliance, recovery, semantic correctness, and privacy leakage assertions.

**RM-COMMS-BENCH-0006:** Reports pin corpus/audience digest, template/render/policy/provider/profile versions, channel/sender/route, locale/time zone, network/device/client matrix, tracking settings, faults, warmup, distributions/confidence, costs, and exact outcome denominators.
