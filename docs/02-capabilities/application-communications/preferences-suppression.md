# Purpose, consent, preferences, and suppression

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0001:** A communication classification binds purpose, topic, transactional/security/operational/legal/promotional class, legal/product policy generation, sender/controller, audience, channels, and required/optional nature.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0002:** Consent, subscription, opt-in/out, channel preference, frequency cap, quiet hours, language, accessibility preference, and mandatory-service exception are distinct scoped assertions with provenance, effective period, and withdrawal/revocation.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0003:** Eligibility derives from classification, endpoint control, consent/preferences, tenant/account state, jurisdiction/product policy, suppression, frequency, quiet hours, emergency/security exceptions, age or protected-class constraints where selected, and evidence freshness.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0004:** Suppressions carry scope/reason/source/effective time/expiry/authority and include opt-out, hard bounce, complaint, invalid token/number, legal block, abuse, recipient request, and administrative hold without conflating meanings.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0005:** Unsubscribe/STOP/preference actions are authenticated or abuse-resistant, idempotent, promptly effective under policy, accessible, language-appropriate, and propagate to every applicable sender/provider/cache while preserving mandatory exceptions explicitly.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0006:** A class label cannot bypass a preference or suppression. Exceptions require named policy, minimal content/channel/frequency, accountable authority, expiry where applicable, and audit.

**RM-COMMS-PREF-0007:** Preference centers expose current scoped state and consequences without dark patterns, bundled consent, preselected optional choices, or requiring unrelated data.
