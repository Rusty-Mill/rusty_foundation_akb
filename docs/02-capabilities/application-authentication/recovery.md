# Recovery and identity re-establishment

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0001:** A recovery case binds subject/account candidate, initiating evidence, reason, policy generation, risk context, selected independent proofs, reviewer/provider actors, delays, notifications, limits, and authority.

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0002:** Recovery distinguishes possession recovery, authenticator replacement, account identity re-establishment, administrative correction, compromised-account containment, and federation/provider restoration. These paths do not share an undifferentiated reset operation.

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0003:** Recovery evidence is evaluated for independence from the lost or compromised authenticator, session, device, email address, phone number, federation source, or administrator. Circular recovery dependencies are detected and reported.

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0004:** Successful recovery creates new authenticator and account-security generations, invalidates or reviews existing authenticators/sessions/tokens/recovery artifacts, applies product-defined cooling-off and transaction restrictions, and sends independent notification.

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0005:** Support personnel receive least disclosure and cannot bypass required proofs, quorum, delay, or audit silently. Exceptions are explicit emergency workflows with bounded consequences and post-event review.

**RM-APP-AUTH-RECOVERY-0006:** Failure and status interfaces resist enumeration and social-engineering amplification while remaining accessible, explainable, and capable of escalation or appeal under product policy.
