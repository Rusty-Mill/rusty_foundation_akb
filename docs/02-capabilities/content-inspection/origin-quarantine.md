# Origin, provenance, and quarantine

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0001:** Origin evidence binds acquisition mechanism, source URI/service/peer where policy permits, referrer, zone/trust class, acquiring principal/application, acquisition time, secure-channel/object generation, and evidence provider/platform generation.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0002:** Quarantine is a typed policy state with reason, scope, source evidence, creator, creation/expiry, allowed operations, review/override requirements, propagation policy, and native mapping; it is not a directory name or universal OS flag.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0003:** Copy, move, rename, download, extraction, mount, synchronization, backup/restore, transform, packaging, and interprocess transfer declare whether origin/quarantine is preserved, merged, strengthened, reconstructed, unsupported, or lost.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0004:** Derived artifacts retain parent lineage and inherit at least the most restrictive relevant origin/quarantine policy unless an authorized policy decision explicitly establishes a different state with evidence.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0005:** Clearing, weakening, or overriding quarantine is a separate auditable authority-bearing effect with user/admin/enterprise mediation. Parse success, malware no-finding, transformation, signature validation, or relocation cannot clear it implicitly.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0006:** Native quarantine and reputation databases may be absent, mutable, user-specific, inconsistent across hard links/copies, or externally changed. Observation binds object identity and time; continuous truth is not claimed.

**RM-CONTENT-ORIGIN-0007:** Sensitive source URIs, referrers, user identities, and browsing/storage history are minimized, partitioned, redacted, retained, and disclosed under explicit privacy policy.
