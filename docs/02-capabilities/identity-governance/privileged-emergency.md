# Privileged and emergency access

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-PRIVILEGE-0001:** Privileged roles are separately classified entitlements with stronger authentication, approval, device/context, duration, session, logging, review, and revocation requirements.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-PRIVILEGE-0002:** Just-in-time elevation creates an expiring assignment generation; expiry triggers revocation and reconciliation rather than relying on a user-interface timer.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-PRIVILEGE-0003:** Emergency access binds incident, requested scope, justification, authorized bypasses, quorum or break-glass policy, expiry, notification, enhanced observation, after-action review, and credential rotation when applicable.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-PRIVILEGE-0004:** Emergency mechanisms remain operable under defined identity-provider or control-plane outages without becoming ambient standing authority. Storage, testing, custody, and use are independently evidenced.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-PRIVILEGE-0005:** Privileged observation avoids capturing unrelated secrets or personal content and protects logs from the actor being observed under product policy.
