# Account lifecycle and joiner-mover-leaver

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-LIFECYCLE-0001:** A lifecycle case binds subject/account/tenant generations, source event, effective time, workflow version, requested changes, owners, approvals, deadlines, authority, effects, exceptions, and closure evidence.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-LIFECYCLE-0002:** Joiner workflows support future-dated staging without premature access, identity proof/correlation, account creation, baseline requests, credential enrollment handoff, ownership, verification, and cancellation.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-LIFECYCLE-0003:** Mover workflows compute removals before additions, enforce separation-of-duty and toxic-combination policy, handle overlap windows explicitly, and re-evaluate ownership, groups, entitlements, sessions, and credentials.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-LIFECYCLE-0004:** Leaver workflows support scheduled and immediate security paths, suspend or disable ingress promptly, revoke active access, transfer or quarantine ownership, preserve required records, and reconcile every downstream boundary.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-LIFECYCLE-0005:** Rehire and correction never reactivate an old generation implicitly; product policy decides correlation, restoration, retained data, reapproval, and credential/session replacement.
