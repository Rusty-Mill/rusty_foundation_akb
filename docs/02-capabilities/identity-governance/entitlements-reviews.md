# Entitlement requests, approvals, and reviews

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-ENTITLEMENT-0001:** Entitlements are typed resource/action/scope descriptors with issuer, generation, owner, risk, prerequisites, incompatibilities, expiry, review policy, and provider mapping; they are distinct from assignments and effective authorization.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-ENTITLEMENT-0002:** Requests bind requester, beneficiary, business purpose, entitlement generation, duration, context, risk evidence, policy result, approver independence, and immutable decision history.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-ENTITLEMENT-0003:** Approval policy handles self-approval, delegation, quorum, conflicts, unavailable approvers, escalation, separation of duties, emergency bypass, expiry, withdrawal, and changed evidence before fulfillment.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-ENTITLEMENT-0004:** Access reviews operate on a versioned campaign scope and evidence snapshot, distinguish retain/revoke/modify/defer/unknown, prevent reviewer conflicts, track nonresponse, and verify downstream revocation.

**RM-IDENTITY-GOV-ENTITLEMENT-0005:** Effective-access reports name their evaluated policy, group graph, assignment frontier, provider observations, freshness, exclusions, unknowns, and resource enforcement boundary; they never claim universal access truth.
